# STAR Prep – interview prep (offline PWA)

A private story bank for behavioural interviews, with spaced-repetition review, question drills in English and German,
a rehearsal timer with optional voice recording, and an interview log.

## Install (once)
1. Repo → **Settings → Pages** → branch `main`, folder `/ (root)` → Save.
2. Open **https://vnsr.github.io/star-prep/** in Safari → Share → **Add to Home Screen**. Open it once while online.

## How to use it
- **Stories:** write each one as Situation, Task, Action, Result, with a one-line headline and the numbers to remember. Tag the competencies it proves. The editor shows the word count and speaking time (aim for 2–2½ minutes, about half of it Action). **Prepared answer** is for non-STAR answers: your pitch, why you are leaving, salary.
- **Practice → Start review:** a question that fits the story, you answer out loud, then check and rate Again / Hard / Good / Easy. Hard stories come back sooner. Before a dated interview nothing is scheduled past the day before it.
- **Random question:** pick which story you would tell, then rehearse it on the timer. "No story yet" means you found a gap.
- **Rehearse:** 1–3 minute timer with Situation & Task → Action → Result phases. **Record my answer** keeps the take on the phone until you close the timer (or **Save to Files**).
- **Interviews:** date, stage, who you met, stories planned and actually told, questions asked. Stories already told to the same company are marked, so the next round gets a fresh one. Questions asked can go into your question bank.
- **Gaps:** competencies with no or one story, and questions you cannot yet answer.

## Sync & backups
Settings → Automatic sync with `username/my-app-data` and your token; STAR Prep writes only to `star/`. Backup to Files and import as in the other apps.
